CIRCLE: "The locus of the plane of points with the same distance r (radius) from a point C(xc,yc) called centre"

Equation of the circle (standard form):

(x-xc)^2 + (y-yc)^2 = r^2

Equation of the circle (general form):

x^2 + y^2 + Ax +By + C = 0

Parametric equation of the circle
x = xc + r * cos(theta), y = yc + r * sin(theta)

if xc=,yc=0
(r*sin(theta))^2+(r*cos(theta))^2=r^2
ex. theta = 90° = pi/2
(r*1)^2 + (r*0)^2 = 3^2

0.5*r^2 + 0.5*r^2 = r^2
r^2 = r^2

Perimeter (circumference) of the circle:

2*pi*r
(pi~=3.141593)

Area of the circle:

pi*r^2
