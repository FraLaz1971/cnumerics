log_a(b) = log_c(b)/log_c(a)

log_a(b) = x

a^x = b

log_c(a^x) = log_c(b)

x*log_c(a) = log_c(b)

x = log_c(b)/log_c(a)

log_a(b) = log_c(b)/log_c(a)



example 
log_10(100) = ln(100)/ln(10)

log_10(100)*ln(10) = ln(100)

e^[log_10(100)*ln(10)] = e^[ln(100)]

[e^(ln(10))]^[log_10^(100)] = 100
10^(log_10^(100)) = 100
10^2 = 100
